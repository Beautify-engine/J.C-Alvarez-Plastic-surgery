# Image treatment — the site grade

Every photograph on the site passes through this before it ships. It is what makes
assets from a dozen different sources read as one commission (§4 — "one grade, one crop
logic, one aspect set").

**Register:** clean premium editorial. Natural, accurate colour with a slight polish —
clean whites, a whisper of black lift, gentle contrast, fine grain, restrained glow.

> **The grade never desaturates.** Cohesion across a mixed set comes from consistent white
> balance, black point and grain — not from removing colour. `clean` retains 100–108% of
> original saturation, measured. The earlier heavily-desaturated presets (`cool`, `air`)
> remain available but are no longer the default; see `docs/DECISIONS.md` D-011.

**Why warm on a cool site:** `--paper` is `#f7f6f3`, an already-warm off-white. Grading
photography to resolve its highlights into that exact value makes images sit *in* the page
rather than on it. The cool notes stay where they belong — the `--ink` bands and the
`--accent` — so the imagery complements the UI instead of competing with it.

## The deterministic route (use this)

```bash
./tools/grade.sh photo.jpg           # standard
./tools/grade.sh photo.jpg air       # hazier, most lifted — light bands
./tools/grade.sh photo.jpg deep      # deeper blacks — hero, gallery, dark bands
./tools/grade.sh assets/shoot/ air   # batch a folder
```

| preset | use | blacks | bloom | grain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **clean** | **DEFAULT — light bands. Natural colour, clean whites.** | slight lift | 0.10 @ σ30 | 9 |
| **clean-deep** | **DEFAULT — dark bands, procedure carousel, gallery.** | deeper, still natural | 0.08 @ σ26 | 8 |
| **air** | heavy warm haze — use sparingly | most lifted, hazy | 0.55 @ σ40 | 16 |
| **standard** | general purpose, team, portraits | balanced | 0.46 @ σ32 | 14 |
| **deep** | dark bands — hero, results gallery | closest to `--ink` | 0.34 @ σ24 | 11 |
| **band** | full-bleed on a dark band — black point == `--ink`, so edges dissolve | matches `#16232a` | 0.30 @ σ22 | 11 |
| **cool** | procedure / body imagery on light bands | cool lift | 0.16 @ σ34 | 12 |
| **cool-deep** | **procedure carousel, body imagery on the dark band** | cool, deepest | 0.13 @ σ26 | 10 |

**Two families.** Warm (`air`/`standard`/`deep`/`band`) for people and place — portraits,
team, facility. Cool (`cool`/`cool-deep`) for procedure and body imagery, where a clinical,
calmer register suits the subject and sits naturally on `--ink`.

Both families **protect skin midtones**: the cast is applied through the black and white
points, not by pushing midtones, because midtones are where skin lives. Pushing them turns
skin grey. The cool family also raises the halation threshold to 0.74 so bright specular
skin does not blow out.

Pick by the band the image sits on. An `air` image on a dark band reads as a grey
rectangle; a `deep` image on `--paper` reads heavy.

### What the chain does, in order

1. **3D LUT** (`design/lut/jc-grade-*.cube`, generated by `tools/make-lut.py` from the
   token values) — desaturate to ~80%, gentle S-curve, warm midtone push toward sand,
   highlight rolloff, then remap the range so **black lands on a warm matte lift and white
   lands on `#f7f6f3`**.
2. **Halation bloom** — isolate highlights above 55%, blur, tint warm, screen back. This
   is the "glow," and it is **spatial**, so no LUT can produce it.
3. **Film grain** — `noise` at 11–16. Measured: 10 is too polite, 20 is the sweet spot,
   34 reads dirty.

Regenerate the LUTs after any token change: `python3 tools/make-lut.py`.

## The prompt route (when an AI tool is doing the edit)

For tools that regrade an image you supply. Less consistent than the LUT — **use it only
where the script cannot reach**, and run the result through `grade.sh deep` afterwards if
it needs to match exactly.

> Regrade this photograph. Keep the composition, subject and framing exactly as they are —
> change only colour, tone and texture.
>
> Warm editorial film look. Lift the blacks so nothing reaches pure black — shadows sit
> matte and slightly warm, never crushed, never blue. Resolve the highlights to a warm
> ivory (#f7f6f3), not clinical white. Push midtones gently toward sand and soft peach so
> skin stays alive and healthy. Overall saturation about 80% of the original, but keep the
> warmth in skin tones.
>
> Low contrast with a gentle film S-curve and a soft highlight rolloff, so bright areas
> compress instead of clipping. Add a subtle warm halation — highlights should bloom and
> bleed slightly into what surrounds them, the way film glows around a bright edge.
> Add fine, even film grain across the whole frame, visible at 100% but never noisy.
>
> The result should feel like a calm, expensive editorial photograph — hazy, warm, quiet.
> Think Hims, Hers, Aesop, Kinfolk.
>
> Do not sharpen. Do not add contrast or vibrance. Do not add colour casts beyond the warm
> shift described. Do not alter the subject, their features, their body or the background
> content. No text, no watermarks, no vignette.

**Order matters:** grade last. Generate or retouch first, grade at the end, so every asset
goes through the same final pass.

## Rules

- **Every shipping image is graded.** An ungraded photo next to graded ones is the single
  most visible failure of §4's "photography treated consistently."
- **Grade before export, not in CSS.** No `filter:` on `<img>` — it costs paint performance
  and breaks under `prefers-reduced-motion` tooling. Bake it in.
- **Never grade the before/after cases.** Clinical results must be colour-accurate. They
  are evidence, not art direction — altering their tone misrepresents an outcome, which is
  a regulatory problem, not a taste one. They sit on the dark band unmodified.
- **Keep the ungraded original** of everything, in `assets/_raw/`.
