# Media brief — what to go and get

Written 2026-08-23. You said you can get **licensed editorial stock** and **his raw
phone / Instagram footage**. This is the shopping list for both, in priority order.

Governing rules, so nothing bought here has to be thrown away:

- **Never a face presented as a patient, a staff member, or a result** (CLAUDE.md §3).
  If a stock person appears, they read as a stock person.
- No "medical blue", no clinic-with-smiling-model, no before/after stock, no syringes,
  no white-coat-arms-folded.
- One grade for everything (D-009 / D-013). Buy raw and neutral; I apply the grade.
- Log every purchase in `assets/MANIFEST.md` with source URL and licence.

## 1 · Licensed stock — the hero problem

The current hero photo is a bright stock close-up of an abdomen being pinched. It is the
single thing capping how premium the page can look. **Buy 3–5 images from this list and
the hero is fixed this week.**

Where: **Stocksy United** (best editorial quality, ~$100–200/image), **Adobe Stock**
(cheaper, more to wade through), **Gallery Stock**, **Trunk Archive** (expensive, film-grade).
Search Stocksy first — it is the closest to the Aesop / Kinfolk register we are targeting.

**Search terms that work:**

| what to search | why |
|---|---|
| `morning light linen bedroom` | recovery, calm, no people — a strong abstract hero |
| `hands detail natural light neutral` | craft and care without a face |
| `minimal clinic interior daylight` | facility register without stock-medical vocabulary |
| `woman 45 window portrait natural back turned` | the demographic, unidentifiable, no result implied |
| `architecture concrete daylight shadow` | texture and section breaks |
| `folded fabric neutral still life` | section dividers, book section, quiet ground |

**Avoid these searches** — they return the vocabulary we are trying to leave behind:
`plastic surgery`, `cosmetic surgery`, `beauty treatment`, `doctor patient consultation`,
`medical spa`, `body contouring`.

**Specs:** ≥3000px on the long edge, no visible watermark, extended/web licence, and
check the model release covers commercial use.

## 2 · His raw footage — free, and better than stock

This is the higher-value half. Ask him for the **camera roll**, not the posts.

| ask | what it unlocks | notes |
|---|---|---|
| **20–30s of B-roll**: gowning, scrubbing, marking (cropped above the shoulders), walking the corridor, at the desk | Replaces the hero on every procedure page | Shot on a recent iPhone is fine. Horizontal, 4K, no filter, don't stop-start |
| **Facility walkthrough**, 60s, slow pan | Exterior, reception, consult room, OR, recovery — all currently `❌` in `ASSET-REQUEST.md` §3 | One walk through the office holding the phone steady |
| **Original masters of his YouTube uploads** | 202 recordings; we currently embed, which is right, but masters let us pull stills and B-roll | YouTube Studio → Download, official export |
| **Unedited Instagram originals** | The posted versions are compressed and have burned-in text | Instagram → Settings → Download Your Information (official export, not scraping) |
| **Anything on his phone of him teaching, with the book, in the OR** | `P3`/`P4` in `ASSET-REQUEST.md`, both `❌` | Even bad phone photos beat stock here — it is really him |

**What makes phone footage usable:** shoot in daylight or near a window, hold still or move
slowly, don't zoom, don't use portrait mode, don't apply a filter. I do the rest.
