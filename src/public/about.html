About

Your result depends on how you heal.

Most surgeons plan the operation. Dr. Julio Clavijo Alvarez plans the months around it.

Before he was a plastic surgeon he was a researcher — a doctorate in general surgery in Spain, then years in a Harvard laboratory and at the University of Pittsburgh studying stem cells, cell culturing and limb transplantation. He arrived in Boston with little English and worked four months unpaid to earn the position.

The route here

Bogotá, a Navy base, Madrid, Boston, Pittsburgh, Miami.

Every entry below is drawn from a 2019 Pittsburgh Post-Gazette profile with named reporting and direct quotes, or from his own published biography.

  1. 1996

    Graduated medical school in Bogotá, Colombia. He had put himself through it partly on a campus business — baking and selling brownies at nineteen, then selling slide-making services to lecturers.

  2. 1997

    A year at a Colombian Navy base on the Pacific coast during the guerrilla conflict. He treated soldiers wounded in combat. In the same year he delivered 180 babies.

  3. 1998

    Moved to Spain, and struggled with the board examinations there — they were technical where his training had been hands-on. He stayed, and earned a doctorate in general surgery focused on cancer treatment.

  4. 2000

    Arrived in Boston on a student visa to work in a research laboratory at Harvard University, speaking little English. He worked four months unpaid to prove he belonged there, and went from assistant to laboratory manager.

    The discrimination I have faced in my time has taught me that, even if you are different, you have to embrace your difference.

    Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, 2019

  5. 2001

    Followed his laboratory head to the University of Pittsburgh — None of the other lab members wanted to move to Pittsburgh. He rose to laboratory manager again, and won funding for work in trauma surgery, critical care and limb transplantation. His research was in stem cells, cell culturing and limb transplantation.

  6. Then

    He passed his boards and began his plastic surgery residency at Pittsburgh — arriving at the speciality with a decade of laboratory work behind him rather than in front of him.

    They told me I could become a surgeon. I could become a researcher, and maybe a businessman. … I have become all three.

    Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, 2019

  7. 2015

    Opened his own practice, ReNova Plastic Surgery and Medical Spa, in Pine, Pennsylvania — financed by a local lender he talked into it. Three years later he moved it into a 3,300 square-foot space in Marshall.

  8. Today

    Miami, Florida — practising at 8400 SW 8th St, and consulting in English and Spanish.

Why it matters to you

Ten years in a laboratory changes what a surgeon worries about.

That is why your surgery starts long before the date. Nutrition, supplements and conditioning in the ninety days before. Hyperbaric oxygen after. A plan that treats healing as part of the procedure — because how your tissue repairs decides how your scars settle, and how soon you look like yourself again.
