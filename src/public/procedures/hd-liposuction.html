One operation, five moves.

One to two hours under general anaesthesia, depending on how many areas. The skill is in what gets left behind, not what comes out.

01 / 05

Or hear him explain it

  1. 01

    Small incisions, hidden in creases

    A few millimetres each, placed in folds and natural lines. The access points are not the scar you worry about with this operation.

  2. 02

    Tumescent solution goes in

    Saline, local anaesthetic and epinephrine. It numbs the field, constricts the vessels so there is far less bleeding, and separates the fat from what sits around it.

  3. 03

    Fat is removed in passes

    Not bulk removal — layered passes at different depths, deeper in some places and deliberately shallow over others. The pattern is the point.

  4. 04

    Definition is revealed

    Fat is left over the muscle bellies and taken from the grooves between them, so the structure underneath reads through the skin. Where it helps, a little fat is grafted back to sharpen a border.

  5. 05

    Drains, then compression

    Small drains take the fluid that would otherwise collect. A faja goes on to control swelling and hold the skin down onto its new contour.

It reveals muscle. It cannot build it.

This operation uncovers the structure you already have by taking the fat off it. If the muscle underneath is not there, there is nothing to uncover and the result is simply thinner. It is his own line — high-definition liposuction is not for everyone — and it is the reason he turns people away from this one more often than from any other.

This is not a weight operation.

It is a contour one. The scales may barely move; what changes is where your outline sits. Someone hoping to lose size is asking the wrong procedure for the wrong thing, and will be disappointed by a technically good result.

Five things he checks.

The same five he works through in the room. A surgeon who operates on everyone who asks is not selecting for results.

Muscle already under there

Likely a candidateYou train, and the structure exists — it is just sitting under a layer that will not shift.

He’ll ask you to waitIf there is nothing underneath yet. This operation uncovers; it does not create. Train first and the result is a different operation.

A weight you can hold

Likely a candidateClose to the weight you mean to keep. Fat cells removed do not return, but the ones left behind still grow.

He’ll ask you to waitIf you are mid-way through losing a significant amount. Finish, then judge what is left.

Skin that will retract

Likely a candidateReasonable skin quality and elasticity, so the surface settles onto the new contour.

He’ll ask you to waitIf the skin is loose enough that it will drape rather than tighten. Removing fat under loose skin makes it looser, and the answer is an excision operation instead.

Three months of compression

Likely a candidateFull-time faja for three months is not a suggestion — it is what holds the skin down while it retracts.

He’ll ask you to waitIf you cannot commit to it. The garment does as much for the final contour as the operation does.

Nicotine-free

Likely a candidateNot smoking, not vaping. Skin needs its blood supply to retract cleanly.

He’ll ask you to waitIf you are still smoking. It slows healing and raises the risk of contour irregularity and fibrosis.

Before you decide.

The risks of this operation are mostly about the surface it leaves behind. Every item below is a genuine possibility, not fine print.

  • Contour irregularityWaves, dents or a patch taken too shallow. The commonest complaint after liposuction, and the hardest to correct — which is why the passes matter more than the volume.
  • FibrosisHard, lumpy scar tissue under the skin as it heals. Massage and time are the answer, and it is why the post-op schedule is not optional.
  • Skin that does not retractIf elasticity was borderline, removing what was filling it can leave it loose. Assessed before booking, not discovered afterwards.
  • Prolonged swellingMonths, not weeks. Anyone promising a quick reveal is selling the first fortnight rather than the result.
  • Fluid collection and infectionManaged with drains, compression and monitoring rather than eliminated.
  • Deep vein thrombosisA clot in the leg. Why walking starts on day one and why smoking is disqualifying.
  • AsymmetryTwo sides were never identical and do not heal identically. Small differences are expected; large ones may need a touch-up.

What it costs

Every quote is built from the same things. Ask for them itemised — with liposuction especially, "areas" is where quotes diverge most and where they are easiest to misread.

  • How many areas, and what counts as one
  • Whether it is a full 360 or front only
  • Whether any fat is grafted back for definition
  • Operating time and anaesthesia
  • Facility fee
  • Garments, drains and the massage schedule

Still asked, often

How is this different from ordinary liposuction?

Ordinary liposuction takes fat off to make an area smaller. This works in layers around the muscles underneath — leaving fat over the muscle bellies and taking it from the grooves between them — so structure reads through the skin.

Does the fat come back?

The cells removed do not return. The ones left behind can still enlarge, so significant weight gain changes the result, and it may do so unevenly.

What is lipo 360?

Treating the whole circumference — abdomen, flanks and back — rather than the front alone. It is what makes a waist read as a waist from every angle rather than only head-on.

Why do I look bigger at two weeks?

Swelling peaks before it falls. The first fortnight routinely looks worse than the before photograph, and it catches people out when nobody warned them.

Do I really need three months of compression?

Yes. The garment holds the skin down onto the new contour while it retracts. It contributes as much to the final shape as the operation does.

Can it be combined with a fat transfer?

Commonly — the fat removed here is the fat used in a Brazilian butt lift, so the two are often the same operation. Which combinations he will and will not do is decided at the consultation.
