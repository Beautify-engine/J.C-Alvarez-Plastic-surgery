Why the form asks so little.

Most consultation forms in this industry ask for your weight, your BMI, your medical history and photographs before anyone has spoken to you.

Collecting health data drags a marketing website into the scope of medical privacy law and creates a breach surface for information the site has no business holding. It also filters people out before a surgeon has had a chance to say anything useful to them.

Clinical detail belongs in the consultation, where someone qualified is asking the questions and can explain why they matter. Four questions is what is needed to get you into that room.
