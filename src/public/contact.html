Please do not email photographs.

Not because they are unwelcome — because an ordinary email inbox is the wrong place for them. Medical history, medication lists, weights and photographs all belong in the consultation, where they are covered by the same rules as the rest of your record.

The consultation form deliberately asks for none of it. Three questions, none of them clinical, and the clinical conversation happens with a surgeon in a room rather than in a marketing form.
