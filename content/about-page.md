## H1

Your result depends on how you heal.

## Lede

Most surgeons plan the operation. Dr. Julio Clavijo Alvarez plans the months around it.

## Body

Before he was a plastic surgeon he was a researcher — a doctorate in general surgery in
Spain, then years in a Harvard laboratory and at the University of Pittsburgh studying
stem cells, cell culturing and limb transplantation. He arrived in Boston with little
English and worked four months unpaid to earn the position.

> They told me I could become a surgeon. I could become a researcher, and maybe a
> businessman. … I have become all three.
>
> — Dr. Alvarez, Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, 2019

That is why your surgery starts long before the date. Nutrition, supplements and
conditioning in the ninety days before. Hyperbaric oxygen after. A plan that treats
healing as part of the procedure — because how your tissue repairs decides how your
scars settle, and how soon you look like yourself again.
